A few specific questions I'd like to include accurately in the guide:

**Extended parking**
Some riders drive to Ridgeland, leave their vehicle at the hotel, take a shuttle or rental car to Natchez, ride the Trace north over 3–4 days, and finish back at TownePlace Suites. Is extended parking available for guests doing this — and is there a daily fee for the parking period when they're not staying?

**Bike box acceptance**
Riders who fly into Jackson (JAN — 17 miles away) often ship their bikes via BikeFlights to the hotel before arrival. Can TownePlace Suites accept a bike box delivery addressed to an arriving guest and hold it until check-in?

**Bike storage**
Is there a secure space — indoor or locked — to store a touring bike overnight?

TownePlace Suites being an extended-stay property makes it a natural fit for this route. Riders finishing the Trace want exactly what you offer: a comfortable place to decompress, a kitchen to cook a real meal, and easy access to JAN for the flight home.

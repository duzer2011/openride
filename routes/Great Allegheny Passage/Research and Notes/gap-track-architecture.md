# Great Allegheny Passage — Phase 2 Track Architecture

**Direction:** Pittsburgh → Cumberland (conventional, preferred)
Climb to Eastern Continental Divide is imperceptible (rail grade <1%).
Final 24 miles into Cumberland are net downhill. Best finish in the portfolio.

## Novice Track — 4 Days (~37-42 mi/day)

- **Day 1:** Pittsburgh → West Newton (36 mi) — flat river riding, urban to rural transition
- **Day 2:** West Newton → Ohiopyle (42 mi) — Yough River corridor, gorge scenery builds
- **Day 3:** Ohiopyle → Meyersdale (42 mi) — climb to continental divide, Big Savage Tunnel
- **Day 4:** Meyersdale → Cumberland (30 mi) — net downhill finish, Keystone Viaduct, Cumberland arrival

**Night 1:** Bright Morning B&B, West Newton — right on trail, multiple buildings for groups
**Night 2:** B&B or hostel in Ohiopyle — book months ahead for summer/fall
**Night 3:** Levi Deal Mansion, Meyersdale — historic, highly rated
**Night 4:** Inn on Decatur or Hampton Inn, Cumberland

---

## Expert Track — 3 Days (~50-62 mi/day)

- **Day 1:** Pittsburgh → Connellsville (62 mi) — long river day, strong legs required
- **Day 2:** Connellsville → Meyersdale (58 mi) — Ohiopyle stop for lunch, continental divide push
- **Day 3:** Meyersdale → Cumberland (30 mi) — easy downhill finish

**Night 1:** Chateau Connellsville — boutique suites in former convent, bike storage
**Night 2:** Levi Deal Mansion, Meyersdale
**Night 3:** Inn on Decatur, Cumberland

## Surface Notes

- First ~15 miles out of Pittsburgh: paved
- MM 15 onward: crushed limestone — any tire 35mm+ handles fine dry, go wider for wet
- 5 tunnels on route — headlight required (Big Savage at 3,294 ft is the main one)
- 20 bridges total
- Wet limestone gets soft — road bikes not recommended

---

## Signature Moments (Guide Copy Anchors)

1. **Big Savage Tunnel** — 914 ft lighted tunnel, trail shares space with Western Maryland Scenic Railroad
2. **Eastern Continental Divide** — 2,392 ft, watershed split between Atlantic and Gulf of Mexico
3. **Ohiopyle Falls** — waterfall visible from trail, Fallingwater (Frank Lloyd Wright) 8 miles by road
4. **Keystone Viaduct** — sweeping curved railroad bridge near Meyersdale
5. **Point State Park Pittsburgh** — where Allegheny meets Monongahela to form the Ohio River
6. **Canal Place Cumberland** — Mile 0 medallion, C&O connection, end-of-trail moment

# Great Allegheny Passage — Phase 2B Vehicle Logistics

---

## Start Point: Pittsburgh, PA (Point State Park — MM 150)

**Address:** Point State Park, 101 Commonwealth Pl, Pittsburgh, PA 15222

**Fly-in scenario (LEAD WITH THIS):**
Pittsburgh International Airport (PIT) → Montour Trail Airport Connector (paved, 21.5 miles) → joins GAP at Point of View Park near McKeesport.
This is the cleanest fly-in story in the entire OpenRide catalog. No shuttle, no taxi, no car — bike from the terminal to the trail.
Note: Airport connector adds ~21 miles before MM 132. Some riders prefer taxi/Lyft to Point State Park and start clean at MM 150.
Weekday option: 28X Airport Flyer bus accepts 2 bikes (Mon-Fri only) to downtown Pittsburgh.

**Car scenario:**
Do NOT park at Point State Park — downtown Pittsburgh parking is expensive for multi-day.
Better: Park at Homestead (MM 139, free long-term lot) or Boston (MM 122, visitor center, well-lit, safe).
Shuttle or Amtrak back to car from Cumberland.

---

## End Point: Cumberland, MD (Canal Place — MM 0)

**Address:** Canal Place Heritage Park, 13 Canal Street, Cumberland, MD 21502

**Return options (in order of preference):**

1. **Amtrak Capitol Limited** — Cumberland → Pittsburgh. Roll-on bike service available. Book early — bike spaces sell out weeks ahead especially summer/fall. (~3.5 hr ride, runs daily)
   Reserve at amtrak.com. Select "bring my bike" at booking — limited spaces per train.

2. **Shuttle services** — Multiple operators serve the GAP corridor. Check gaptrail.org for current list. Prices vary, typically $75-150 per person + bike depending on distance.

3. **Western Maryland Scenic Railroad** — Steam train Cumberland → Frostburg (not Pittsburgh). Scenic add-on, not a return solution.

## Getting There & Getting Home (Draft Section)

**Getting to the Start**
The GAP has the best fly-in story of any trail in this guide series. Pittsburgh International Airport is connected to the trail by the Montour Trail Airport Connector — 21.5 miles of paved path from the terminal to the GAP. Land, unbox your bike, and you're already riding. If you'd rather start fresh at Point State Park (MM 150), a Lyft from PIT takes about 30 minutes.

**Getting Home from Cumberland**
The Amtrak Capitol Limited runs daily from Cumberland back to Pittsburgh. It takes roll-on bikes, takes about 3.5 hours, and drops you back where you started. Book the bike space when you buy your ticket — it sells out. This is the return trip. There is no better-designed trail logistics story than riding 150 miles and taking the train home.

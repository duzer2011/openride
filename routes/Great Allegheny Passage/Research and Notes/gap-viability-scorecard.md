# Great Allegheny Passage — Phase 1 Viability Scorecard

**Route:** Pittsburgh, PA → Cumberland, MD
**Distance:** 150 miles
**Surface:** Crushed limestone rail trail (paved first 15 miles out of Pittsburgh)
**Scored:** March 2026

---

## Scoring

| Cat | Category | Score | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Traffic Safety | 5/5 | Fully separated trail, zero road miles on core route. No cars. |
| 2 | Scenery & Experience Quality | 5/5 | USA Today Top 10 trails. Ohiopyle gorge, Big Savage Tunnel, Eastern Continental Divide, river valleys throughout. |
| 3 | Lodging Availability | 4/5 | Trail towns every 20-35 miles. Cumberland, Frostburg, Meyersdale, Confluence, Ohiopyle, Connellsville, West Newton, Pittsburgh all have B&Bs and/or hotels. One closure flag noted below. |
| 4 | Food & Water Access | 4/5 | Every trail town has restaurants. Ohiopyle, Connellsville, West Newton all have trailside dining. Minor gaps between towns require carrying snacks. |
| 5 | Navigation Simplicity | 5/5 | One trail, point-to-point, mile markers the entire length. Pittsburgh end has minor urban wayfinding complexity but manageable. |
| 6 | Logistical Viability | 4/5 | PIT airport connects via Montour Trail (21.5 mi paved) directly to GAP. Amtrak Capitol Limited serves both endpoints with roll-on bike service. Multiple shuttle operators available. Car parking at Homestead (MM 139) or Boston (MM 122). |
| 7 | Distance Tier Viability | 4/5 | Novice: 4 days (~37-42 mi/day). Expert: 3 days (~50-62 mi/day). Beginner: Pittsburgh→Ohiopyle standalone 2-day (71 mi) viable with lodging at West Newton midpoint. All tiers supported. |

**Total: 31/35 — FLAGSHIP tier**

## Decision

**BUILD. Flagship-tier route.** Second build after Natchez Trace South.
Logistics story (fly PIT → ride to Cumberland → Amtrak home) is the strongest in the portfolio.
No car required. Zero road miles. Trail towns every day.

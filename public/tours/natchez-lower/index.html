About this ride

The best Trace day of the tour. Seven miles east on MS 552 (the first 5 from Alcorn have a wide shoulder) to the Trace at MP 30. Then 30 miles south to the end of the parkway, and about 2.5 miles into town.

The road rolls, with about 1,800 feet of climbing.

Road notes

  • Coles Creek (MP 17.5) has restrooms and a bike repair station.
  • Mount Locust (MP 15.5): the old inn building is closed for structural repairs. The grounds are open.

Questions

Why start on a Sunday and finish on a Saturday?
The Enterprise office in Natchez is closed Sundays, which rules out a Sunday one-way rental home, and Vicksburg has few dinner options on Sunday nights. Sunday to Saturday also puts the Trace days on weekdays, when traffic is lighter.
Is the whole route on the Natchez Trace Parkway?
No. About half is on the parkway and half is on state and county roads. Everything is paved. The parkway has a 50 mph limit, 11-foot lanes and no shoulder. The state roads are a mix, and the tour page flags the stretches where the shoulder or traffic is unconfirmed.
What is the best time of year to ride it?
March to April and October. Summer heat and humidity make July and August hard going, and spring brings rain. Book inns 6 to 8 weeks ahead for the good months, because Canemount has only six rooms.
How do I get home from Natchez?
Fly out of Natchez to Houston, pick up an Enterprise one-way rental to Jackson airport by noon on Saturday, or book a van ahead. Greyhound to Baton Rouge and shipping your bike home are the other options. The tour page lists each one with phone numbers and costs.
Can I ride it on an e-bike?
Yes, with planning. Day 3 is the range test, with up to 60 miles of rolling terrain. Charge fully in Vicksburg and ask Canemount about an outlet for overnight charging. Vicksburg National Military Park allows e-bikes under 750W, but not riding on the motor alone.
Is there luggage transfer between inns?
Not confirmed for this route. Until one is found, plan to carry your own gear in panniers or a trunk bag, or have a friend drive it.
Can I camp instead of staying in inns?
Camping is thin on this route. Natchez State Park has tent sites about 10 miles north of town, and Rocky Springs has no drinkable water. This tour is built around inns. A camping version needs its own chain of stops.
How much does a self-guided Natchez Trace tour cost?
It depends on where you sleep. Camping keeps it low; inn-to-inn with historic B&Bs runs roughly $150-260 a night per room. Add food, getting to the start in Jackson, and getting home from Natchez. Access to this page includes every inn with its price range.
